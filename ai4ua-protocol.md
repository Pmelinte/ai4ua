## 2. The 3-Entity Core Matrix (9 Multi-AI Mesh)
Every audit request runs sequentially through 3 independent entities. [cite_start]Each entity is a council made of 3 distinct AI models running in parallel[cite: 1047, 1048, 1421]:
1. [cite_start]**American Model** (e.g., Anthropic/OpenAI) [cite: 1432]
2. [cite_start]**Chinese Model** (e.g., DeepSeek/Qwen) [cite: 1434]
3. [cite_start]**Open-Source Model** (e.g., Llama/Mistral) [cite: 1433]

[cite_start]The score of each individual entity is a weighted average based on the inner confidence metrics of these 3 models[cite: 1454].

### Entity 1: The Architect (Defines Metrics)
- [cite_start]Analyzes the specific industry layout[cite: 436, 1551].
- [cite_start]Formulates 10 to 50 objective operational metrics unique to that context (e.g., hidden clauses for insurance, bot walls for SaaS, cancellation flow friction for retail)[cite: 107, 176, 204, 437, 488, 526].

### Entity 2: The Executor (Measures Parameters)
- [cite_start]Consumes structured forms and public documents uploaded by the user[cite: 212, 542, 550].
- [cite_start]Measures the precise alignment between marketing promises and contract/operational reality[cite: 669, 720, 967, 1022].

### Entity 3: The Censor (Audits and Validates)
- [cite_start]Acts as an adversarial reviewer[cite: 1465].
- [cite_start]Checks for model bias, verification errors, and workflow abnormalities[cite: 1097, 1101, 1460, 1462].

## 3. Dynamic Switch & Role Rotation
- [cite_start]**The Anti-Pact Mechanism**: To eliminate long-term algorithmic monoculture or collusion, roles are systematically rotated[cite: 1123, 1415].
- **Trigger**: Every $X$ successful enrolments within a specific industry, a role rotation occurs automatically.
- **Switch Action**:
  - Entity 1 becomes the Censor (3)
  - Entity 2 becomes the Architect (1)
  - Entity 3 becomes the Executor (2)
- [cite_start]All historical data for that batch is re-validated under the new role configuration to test consensus convergence and output absolute trust thresholds[cite: 1477, 1494].
