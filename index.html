How It Works

From incident to score in 4 steps

No human judges. No opinion polls. A structured pipeline from evidence to objective output.

01
Structured incident submitted
User reports facts, not feelings. No free-text rants. Typed fields force evidence-based reporting.
02
AI credibility check
The user's own trust score is verified. Patterns of false reporting are filtered automatically.
03
9-model audit panel
Three AI pillars (Western, Asian, Open Source) independently analyse the evidence. Divergence is measured.
04
Score updated with trend
Company score adjusts based on severity, pattern frequency, and whether the issue was resolved.
